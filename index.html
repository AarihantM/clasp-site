A pocket diary for iPhone

Clasp

Open your iPhone Duo, and your diary opens to today.

A leather-bound diary with one dated page for every day: today on the right, yesterday on the left. Close your phone and the cover clasps shut. On any other iPhone, it shows one page at a time.

  • iPhone Duo and any iPhone with iOS 27 or later
  • No accounts, ads or tracking

Made like a real diary

Everything a paper diary does, and a few things it can't.

Clasp is a single purchase. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to subscribe to.

Opens with your phone

On iPhone Duo, unfolding the phone opens the diary to today, with leather and clasp sounds. Fold it shut and the cover closes.

Real pages

Ruled or blank paper, handwriting-style ink, pages that curl as you turn them, corners you can fold down, and a satin ribbon.

Leather that ages

The cover darkens and wears gently the more you write, and no two diaries wear the same way. Prefer it new? Turn ageing off.

Find any day

Browse a month calendar with a mark on every day you wrote, search across every page, and jump straight to it.

Locked when you want

Lock the diary with Touch ID, Face ID or your passcode. Tap the brass clasp on the cover to open it.

Keep it on paper

Export any week or month as a PDF, drawn from the same pages you wrote, to print or keep.

Private by design

Your diary stays yours.

Your pages stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, where they sync between your devices. There's no Clasp account and no server of mine in between, so I can't read a word.
