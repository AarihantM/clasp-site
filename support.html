Clasp, a pocket diary for iPhone

Clasp is a leather pocket diary with one dated page for each day. On iPhone Duo, opening the phone opens the diary to today, with yesterday on the left; closing it shuts the cover. On any other iPhone with iOS 27 or later, it shows one page at a time. You can search your pages, browse a month calendar, lock the diary, and export a week or month as a PDF. Your diary syncs through your own iCloud.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email asm.business03@gmail.com. Tell me your iPhone model, iOS version and Clasp version (shown at the bottom of Clasp's Settings). Please don't include diary entries unless you want me to read them.

Questions

How does syncing work?

Clasp saves your diary to your own private iCloud storage and syncs it to every iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account. There's no Clasp account and no server of mine in between; I can't see your entries.

If pages aren't syncing:

  1. Check every device is signed in with the same Apple Account.
  2. In the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and make sure Clasp is turned on in the list of apps using iCloud.
  3. Make sure you're online and iCloud storage isn't full.
  4. Open Clasp and give it a minute. Changes made offline upload when you're back online.

If a blank day gets written on two devices before they sync, Clasp keeps both texts. If you edit the same page on two devices while one is offline, the edit that syncs last can replace the other, so finish a page on one device before writing on another.

Before you turn iCloud off for Clasp or sign out of iCloud: iOS removes the synced pages from that iPhone. They stay in your iCloud and should come back when you turn it on again. Pages written while offline just before may not have uploaded yet, so connect to the internet and open Clasp for a minute first.

How do I lock my diary?
  1. Open Clasp and tap Settings (the sliders button in the toolbar).
  2. Under Privacy, turn on Lock with Touch ID (or Face ID / passcode, depending on your iPhone).
  3. Clasp asks you to unlock once to confirm. Then choose when it relocks: When closed or Leaving app. On iPhones that show one page at a time, leaving the app always relocks.

Your iPhone needs a passcode set first. To unlock, tap the brass clasp on the cover, then use Touch ID, Face ID or your passcode. On iPhone Duo the fingerprint is read by the side button; the pad on the screen only starts the request. If Touch ID or Face ID fails, you can use your passcode, so you're never locked out. Clasp never sees your fingerprint, face or passcode, and while the lock is on it hides your pages in the app switcher.

The lock is set separately on each iPhone. Turn it on on every device that has your diary.

The lock keeps the diary closed in Clasp. It doesn't encrypt anything by itself; see the privacy policy for how iCloud protects your pages.

My iPhone doesn't fold. Can I still use Clasp?

Yes. On any iPhone with iOS 27 or later, Clasp shows one page at a time with a leather spine on the left. Tap the cover to open the diary, swipe or use the arrows to turn pages, and tap the spine to close it. Everything else (sync, lock, search, export) works the same.

How do I export my diary?
  1. On an open iPhone Duo, tap Export as PDF in the toolbar. On any iPhone, you can also tap Settings (the sliders button), then Export as PDF….
  2. Pick Week or Month and use the arrows to choose which one. Turn on Include blank days if you want empty pages too.
  3. Tap Create PDF, then choose where to send it: Save to Files, Mail, Print or another app.

The PDF is made on your iPhone in your own ink and paper. Clasp deletes its temporary copy afterwards.

How do I back up my diary?
  • iCloud (automatic): with iCloud on for Clasp, your diary is kept in your iCloud and can come back on a new iPhone when you sign in and install Clasp, once your pages have uploaded (that needs an internet connection and free iCloud storage).
  • iPhone backups: iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer also includes Clasp.
  • Your own copy: export each month as a PDF and save it to Files or a computer.
How do I delete my diary?
  • A page: in Clasp, select the page's text and delete it; drag a folded corner back to unfold it. This syncs to your other devices. The empty page stays in place for its date.
  • From one iPhone: delete the Clasp app. If you use iCloud, your diary stays there and comes back if you reinstall.
  • Everywhere: open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud > Storage (or Manage Account Storage). If Clasp is listed, tap it, then Delete Data from iCloud. Then delete the app from each device. This can't be undone, so export anything you want to keep first. If you can't find Clasp there, email me and I'll help.

I can't see or delete your diary myself: it's held only on your devices and in your iCloud.

I lost my entries. What can I do?
  1. Wait a moment. On a new or reinstalled iPhone, pages can take a few minutes to download from iCloud. Keep Clasp open and stay online.
  2. Check the Apple Account. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used before, and that iCloud is on for Clasp (Settings > your name > iCloud).
  3. Check the date. Use Contents (the list button) to see the calendar; days with writing have an ink dot. Search finds any word you remember.
  4. Restore a backup. If you had iCloud off, your diary lived only on that iPhone. An iPhone backup made before the loss may still contain it.

Your diary is held only on your devices and in your iCloud, so I can't see or restore it for you. If none of this helps, email me and I'll do what I can to guide you.
