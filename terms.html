The lock

The lock is a convenience. When it's on, Clasp asks iOS to check your Touch ID, Face ID or passcode before it shows your pages, and it hides your pages in the app switcher. It doesn't encrypt your diary, it doesn't change how your diary is stored, and anyone who knows your iPhone's passcode can open it. It isn't a guarantee that no one else can read your diary. Keep your iPhone's passcode private.

The lock is set separately on each iPhone. Turn it on on every device that has your diary.
